Where Butterflies Live

Butterflies can be found in a wide range of habitats including forests, grasslands, wetlands, deserts, and even urban gardens. They thrive in environments that provide nectar sources and host plants for laying eggs.

Monarch Migration

Monarch butterflies complete one of the longest migrations of any insect, traveling up to 3,000 miles between North America and central Mexico.

They rely heavily on milkweed plants for reproduction and survival. Habitat loss and climate change are major threats to their journey.

Climate Influence

Climate plays a critical role in butterfly survival. Temperature changes, shifting seasons, and extreme weather events can disrupt migration timing, food availability, and breeding patterns.

As global temperatures rise, many butterfly species are shifting their ranges or experiencing population declines.
